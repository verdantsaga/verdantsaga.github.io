# Verdant Jedi Order

#jedi #government #military #canon

## Overview
The Verdant Jedi Order serves as the primary Force-sensitive institution of the [[Verdant Republic]]. Unlike the Jedi Order of the old Galactic Republic, the Verdant Jedi Order evolved alongside a heavily militarized and socially integrated Republic, resulting in major ideological and structural differences from traditional Jedi doctrine.

The Order is deeply connected to Verdant society and operates as both a spiritual institution and an active defensive force within the Republic. Verdant Jedi commonly serve as military officers, advisors, diplomats, strategists, investigators, and protectors of the Republic.

One of the defining characteristics of the Verdant Jedi Order is its belief that Jedi should live among the people rather than separate themselves from society. This philosophy led to the removal of many traditional restrictions, including the prohibition on personal attachments.

The Order is headquartered across four major fortified temples rather than a single centralized location.

# History

The modern Verdant Jedi Order emerged during a period known as the Great Jedi Surge, a major increase in Force-sensitive activity and Jedi recruitment on Verdant’s capital world.

As the Republic expanded and external threats intensified, the Founder of Verdant encouraged closer integration between the Jedi and the Republic military. This resulted in many Jedi entering military service directly as officers within the [[Verdant Republic Military]].

The creation of the modern Order occurred before the Republic’s first direct engagement with the Empire and permanently reshaped the role of Jedi within Verdant society.

Unlike the old Galactic Republic, Verdant leadership intentionally avoided centralizing the Jedi into a single massive temple complex. Military planners believed a centralized structure would create a strategic vulnerability capable of crippling the entire Order if destroyed.

Instead, the Republic constructed four heavily fortified Jedi temples across multiple secure regions of Republic space.

# Doctrine

The Verdant Jedi Order follows many traditional Jedi teachings regarding:
- self-discipline,
- service,
- protection of civilians,
- and resistance to corruption through the dark side.

However, several major philosophical differences separate the Order from the older Jedi traditions.

## Emotional Philosophy

The Verdant Jedi believe:
*"Emotion must be mastered, not suppressed."*

Unlike the traditional Jedi Order, Verdant doctrine does not forbid emotional attachment, family relationships, or personal connections. The Order teaches that emotional understanding and discipline are healthier than emotional denial.

As a result:
- marriage is permitted,
- families are permitted,
- Jedi may maintain civilian lives,
- and some Jedi retire from active service entirely.

This philosophy has made the Order significantly more integrated into Republic society.

# Military Integration

One of the most defining aspects of the Verdant Jedi Order is its direct military integration.

Most Jedi serve within the Republic military as commissioned officers. Jedi frequently command:
- infantry formations,
- fleets,
- reconnaissance units,
- special operations groups,
- and defensive task forces.

Common Jedi military assignments include:
- Battlefield Commanders
- Naval Officers
- Intelligence Advisors
- Reconnaissance Leaders
- Diplomats
- Temple Security Coordinators
- Strategic Advisors

Jedi officers are expected to complete formal military training alongside traditional Jedi instruction.

# The Four Temples

The Verdant Jedi Order operates from four major fortified temple complexes instead of a single grand temple.

This structure was designed to:
- prevent catastrophic losses,
- preserve Jedi archives,
- maintain regional stability,
- and reduce the risk of a single successful attack destroying the Order.

Each temple maintains its own internal leadership and specialized responsibilities while remaining loyal to the High Council of the Order.

The temples are heavily defended by:
- Republic military garrisons,
- [[Verdant Guards]],
- and [[Verdant Knights]].

# Gray Jedi Influence

A significant number of Verdant Jedi are considered Gray Jedi by more traditional standards.

These Jedi are known for:
- aggressive battlefield tactics,
- pragmatic decision-making,
- and willingness to use lethal force against hostile threats.

Some Jedi within the Order believe that excessive mercy during wartime can prolong suffering and increase civilian casualties. Others strongly oppose these views and fear the growing militarization of the Order.

This ideological divide remains one of the most controversial issues within the modern Jedi Order.

Although executions of prisoners and extreme actions are officially discouraged, some wartime incidents involving aggressive Jedi commanders have been quietly tolerated by Republic leadership during periods of severe conflict.

# Relationship With the Republic

The relationship between the Jedi and the Republic is exceptionally close.

Unlike the old Republic, where many civilians viewed Jedi as distant monks or political outsiders, Verdant society largely sees Jedi as equal citizens and defenders of the Republic.

Republic soldiers commonly fight alongside Jedi officers and frequently place themselves at risk to protect them during combat operations. This mutual respect created a strong culture of trust between the military and the Order.

Many civilians view the Jedi as:
- protectors,
- military heroes,
- diplomats,
- and symbols of sacrifice.

The Order’s close relationship with the Republic has also increased political pressure on the Jedi during times of war.

# Relationship With The Military

The Verdant Jedi Order maintains close operational cooperation with:
- [[Verdant Republic Ground Forces]]
- [[Verdant Republic Navy]]
- [[Air Cavalry]]
- [[Republic Intelligence Command]]
- [[Expeditionary Threat Command]]
- [[Verdant Guards]]
- [[Verdant Knights]]

Joint military operations involving Jedi officers are considered standard throughout the Republic Armed Forces.

Jedi are especially common within:
- command staff,
- reconnaissance operations,
- defensive task forces,
- and strategic response units.

# Temple Security

Following multiple assassination attempts and external threats against the Order, temple security became a major Republic priority.

The temples are protected by:
- fortified military defenses,
- anti-ship weapon systems,
- armored security divisions,
- Republic fleet patrols,
- and dedicated [[Verdant Knights]] detachments.

The Jedi temples are considered among the most secure locations within Republic territory.

# Culture

The culture of the Verdant Jedi Order is heavily influenced by both traditional Jedi teachings and Verdant military society.

Common cultural traits include:
- strong loyalty to the Republic,
- disciplined emotional control,
- military professionalism,
- respect for civilian life,
- and belief in personal responsibility.

Many Jedi maintain close ties with local communities and often participate directly in civilian relief operations, reconstruction projects, and humanitarian missions.

Because Jedi are permitted to live more openly among the population, they are significantly more visible within daily Republic life than Jedi of earlier eras.

# Reputation

The Verdant Jedi Order is widely respected throughout Republic space.

To Republic citizens, the Jedi are viewed as:
- defenders of peace,
- protectors of Verdant,
- and symbols of sacrifice and unity.

Enemy forces often regard the Order as one of the Republic’s greatest strategic strengths due to the combination of Force abilities and military leadership experience possessed by many Jedi officers.

Despite their respected status, concerns regarding militarization and Gray Jedi influence continue to generate debate both inside and outside the Order.

# Motto

*"In Service Through Balance."*
