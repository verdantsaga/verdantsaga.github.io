## Organizational Hierarchy

| Formation  | Personnel                       | Commander                         |
| ---------- | ------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Army Group | Hundreds of millions → billions | Field Marshal                     |
| Field Army | 5-50 million                    | General / Lt. General             |
| Corps      | 500,000-5 Million               | Lt. General / Major General       |
| Legion     | 100,000–500,000                 | Major General                     |
| Division   | 20,000–80,000                   | Major General / Brigadier General |
| Brigade    | 5,000–20,000                    | Colonel                           |
| Battalion  | 800–3,000                       | Lt. Colonel / Major               |
| Company    | 120–400                         | Major / Captain                   |
| Platoon    | 30–60                           | Lieutenant                        |
| Squad      | 8–12                            | Sergeant                          |
| Fireteam   | 4-6                             | Corporal                          |
