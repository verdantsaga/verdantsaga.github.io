# Republic Intelligence Command

#military #intelligence #classified #canon

## Overview
The Republic Intelligence Command (RIC) serves as the primary military intelligence organization of the [[Verdant Republic]]. Responsible for strategic intelligence gathering, battlefield reconnaissance, counterintelligence, operational analysis, and threat assessment, the Command provides intelligence support across all branches of the [[Verdant Republic Armed Forces]].

RIC plays a critical role in maintaining the Republic’s strategic awareness during large-scale military operations and interstellar conflicts.

## Responsibilities
The Republic Intelligence Command is responsible for:
- battlefield intelligence
- strategic reconnaissance
- counterintelligence operations
- enemy force analysis
- military surveillance
- signals intelligence
- operational threat assessment
- covert intelligence support

## Authority
RIC operates under the authority of:
- [[High Strategic Council]]

and coordinates extensively with:
- [[Republic Special Operations Command]]
- [[Verdant Republic Navy]]
- [[Verdant Republic Ground Forces]]
- [[Air Cavalry]]

## Operational Philosophy
RIC prioritizes:
- strategic information dominance
- compartmentalized intelligence systems
- rapid threat analysis
- proactive surveillance
- operational secrecy

The Command is heavily focused on preventing strategic surprise and maintaining continuous intelligence coverage across contested regions.

## Classification Structure
Many RIC programs operate under:
- restricted access protocols
- compartmentalized clearance systems
- black-level classification authority

Certain intelligence divisions reportedly function without direct Senate oversight during wartime conditions.

## Known Divisions
- [[Signals Intelligence Division]]
- [[Counterintelligence Bureau]]
- [[Deep Recon Division]]
- [[Strategic Threat Analysis Office]]

## Relationship With The Senate
The relationship between RIC and the [[Verdant Senate]] has occasionally become politically controversial due to:
- classified surveillance programs
- wartime secrecy laws
- emergency intelligence authorities
- restricted operational disclosure

Several senators have expressed concern regarding the growing influence of military intelligence during periods of prolonged conflict.

## Relationship With General Kennedy
RIC maintains a close operational relationship with [[General Kennedy]] and frequently provides strategic intelligence directly to Republic high command during major military campaigns.

## Major Operations
RIC played significant roles during:
- [[Virex Campaign]]
- [[Imperial Border Conflicts]]

## Reputation
Within the Republic military, RIC is known for:
- operational secrecy
- strategic efficiency
- aggressive intelligence gathering
- extensive surveillance capabilities

Imperial intelligence agencies reportedly classify RIC as one of the Republic’s most dangerous strategic assets.
