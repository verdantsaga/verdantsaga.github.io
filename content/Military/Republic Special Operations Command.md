# Republic Special Operations Command

#military #specialforces #classified #canon

## Overview
The Republic Special Operations Command (RSOC) serves as the elite unconventional warfare and strategic special operations branch of the [[Verdant Republic Armed Forces]].

RSOC is responsible for:
- covert warfare
- direct action raids
- counterinsurgency
- strategic reconnaissance
- hostage rescue
- black operations
- high-value target elimination
- deep infiltration missions

Due to the classified nature of many RSOC operations, much of the organization remains hidden from the general public and even portions of the Republic government.

## Oversight
RSOC operates under the authority of:
- [[High Strategic Council]]

Many operations are coordinated alongside:
- [[Republic Intelligence Bureau]]
- [[Verdant Senate]]
- [[Black Archive Initiative]]

## Operational Philosophy
RSOC prioritizes:
- operational secrecy
- rapid deployment
- independent unit action
- strategic flexibility
- precision strike capability

## Known Subdivisions
- [[Republic Ranger Corps]]
- [[Specter Division]]
- [[Special Recon Groups]]
- [[Combat Infiltration Units]]

## Recruitment
Personnel are selected from:
- veteran Ground Forces units
- Air Cavalry formations
- intelligence services
- elite legion personnel

Selection standards are considered among the most difficult within the Republic military.
