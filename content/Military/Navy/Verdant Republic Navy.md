# Verdant Republic Navy

#military #organization #canon

## Overview
The Verdant Republic Navy serves as the primary interstellar warfare branch of the [[Verdant Republic Armed Forces]]. Responsible for fleet combat operations, strategic defense, orbital superiority, and long-range force projection, the Navy forms the backbone of Republic military power throughout known space.

The Navy is widely regarded as one of the largest and most technologically advanced military fleets in the region.

## Responsibilities
The Navy is responsible for:
- interstellar fleet warfare
- orbital dominance
- planetary blockade operations
- strategic force projection
- convoy escort and logistics protection
- deep space patrol operations
- rapid deployment support

## Command Structure
The Navy operates under the authority of:
- [[High Strategic Council]]

and the overall command structure of:
- [[Verdant Republic Armed Forces]]

Civilian oversight and funding are coordinated through:
- [[Verdant Senate]]

## Doctrine
The Verdant Republic Navy emphasizes:
- overwhelming fleet coordination
- layered naval defense
- rapid strategic mobility
- sustained logistical warfare
- orbital superiority
- combined fleet-ground operations

Republic naval doctrine prioritizes securing orbital control before major planetary offensives begin.

## Fleet Organization
The Navy is divided into:
- sector fleets
- expeditionary fleets
- carrier strike groups
- orbital assault fleets
- logistics support commands

## Known Fleets
- [[1st Expeditionary Fleet]]
- [[3rd Defense Fleet]]
- [[Outer Rim Response Fleet]]

## Naval Infantry
Boarding operations, orbital assaults, and ship security operations are frequently conducted by:
- [[Republic Expeditionary Marines]]

## Relationship With Other Branches
The Navy works closely alongside:
- [[Air Cavalry]]
- [[Verdant Republic Ground Forces]]
- [[Verdant Republic Logistics Corps]]

## Military Industry
The Navy relies heavily on the Republic’s vast industrial infrastructure for:
- warship construction
- fleet maintenance
- naval logistics
- carrier production
- orbital defense systems

## Relationship With The Senate
Due to increasing fears regarding the [[Galactic Empire]], the [[Verdant Senate]] frequently supports:
- naval expansion programs
- emergency shipbuilding initiatives
- fleet modernization acts
- strategic defense appropriations

This has led to rapid naval growth during periods of heightened galactic tension.

## Operational History
The Navy played major roles during:
- [[Clone Wars Era]]
- [[Virex Campaign]]
- [[Imperial Border Conflicts]]

## Reputation
The Verdant Republic Navy is known for:
- strict discipline
- operational coordination
- industrial scale warfare
- strategic adaptability
- powerful expeditionary capability

Imperial intelligence agencies classify the Navy as a major regional strategic threat.
