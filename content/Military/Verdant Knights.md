# Verdant Knights

#military #eliteforces #canon

## Overview
The Verdant Knights are an elite military order within the [[Verdant Republic Military]] tasked with the protection of high-value command personnel, strategic assets, government facilities, and Jedi installations. Unlike the [[Verdant Jedi Order]], the Verdant Knights are not Force-sensitive warriors, but highly trained veteran soldiers specializing in advanced close-quarters combat and lightsaber warfare.

The order was created following the Great Jedi Surge on Verdant after military leadership recognized the growing need for specialized personnel capable of defending Jedi officials, Republic command centers, and critical infrastructure against hostile Force-sensitive threats, assassins, and infiltration units.

The Verdant Knights are considered among the most disciplined and respected soldiers within the Republic and are often viewed as the military counterpart to the Jedi Order.

# Role Within the Republic

The Verdant Knights serve as:
- High-value command protection units
- Temple security personnel
- Strategic asset defense forces
- Anti-assassin specialists
- Boarding and close-quarters combat teams
- Emergency response detachments
- Honor guards for senior Republic leadership

Verdant Knights frequently operate alongside:
- [[Verdant Guards]]
- [[Verdant Republic Ground Forces]]
- [[Republic Intelligence Command]]
- [[Verdant Jedi Order]]

Although they maintain close ties with the Jedi Order, the Knights remain under direct military authority.

# Recruitment & Selection

Recruitment into the Verdant Knights is highly selective.

Minimum requirements include:
- Rank of Sergeant or higher
- Prior service in the regular military or Special Forces
- Combat deployment experience
- Recommendation from commanding officers
- Exceptional close-quarters combat proficiency
- Advanced psychological screening
- Loyalty and discipline evaluations

Many recruits originate from:
- Special operations units
- Verdant Guards
- Naval boarding detachments
- Air Cavalry security teams
- Republic military police tactical divisions

Only a small percentage of applicants successfully complete Knight training.

# Training

Verdant Knight training is regarded as one of the most difficult military qualification programs within the Republic.

Training includes:
- Advanced lightsaber combat
- Hand-to-hand combat
- VIP protection doctrine
- Defensive formation combat
- Counter-insurgency tactics
- Anti-Force-user engagement training
- Boarding operations
- Urban warfare
- Temple defense operations
- Tactical coordination with Jedi officers

Unlike Jedi training, Verdant Knight doctrine focuses heavily on:
- discipline,
- coordination,
- tactical positioning,
- and defensive combat operations.

Their combat methods are structured and militaristic rather than acrobatic or Force-assisted.

# Equipment

Verdant Knights are equipped with specialized combat armor designed for defensive and close-quarters operations.

Standard equipment includes:
- Reinforced combat armor
- Ceremonial combat robes or tabards
- Military-grade lightsabers
- Blaster sidearms
- Combat shields
- Tactical communication systems
- Saber-resistant armor plating

Their lightsabers are engineered specifically for military reliability and prolonged combat operations rather than Jedi ceremonial traditions.

# Combat Doctrine

Verdant Knights specialize in:
- defensive warfare,
- corridor fighting,
- command bunker defense,
- shipboard security,
- and protection operations.

Unlike the Jedi, who often fight individually, Verdant Knights commonly operate in coordinated formations and tactical teams.

Combat doctrine emphasizes:
- defensive positioning,
- coordinated strikes,
- suppression tactics,
- and protection of assigned personnel.

Their battlefield presence is intended to stabilize critical sectors rather than pursue offensive campaigns.

# Relationship With the Jedi

The relationship between the Verdant Knights and the [[Verdant Jedi Order]] is historically close but occasionally strained.

Many Jedi view the Knights as honorable defenders of the Republic and value their loyalty and discipline. Others question the militarization of lightsaber combat outside traditional Jedi teachings.

Verdant Knights are trained to respect Jedi authority during joint operations but remain part of the formal Republic military chain of command.

Joint deployments between Jedi officers and Verdant Knight detachments are common during major military operations.

# Reputation

Within the Republic, the Verdant Knights are viewed as symbols of discipline, sacrifice, and loyalty.

Republic civilians often associate the Knights with:
- security,
- protection,
- and unwavering service.

Enemy forces regard them as highly dangerous close-combat specialists capable of defending key Republic positions under extreme conditions.

Their presence around military headquarters, Senate facilities, and Jedi temples is considered a sign of strategic importance.

# Organization

The Verdant Knights operate under the authority of the [[Verdant Guards]].

Major divisions include:
- Temple Security Division
- Command Protection Division
- Strategic Defense Division
- Fleet Security Division
- Honor Guard Detachments

Knight detachments are commonly assigned to:
- Republic command ships
- Senate facilities
- Jedi temples
- military headquarters
- strategic research installations
