# Air Cavalry

#military #organization #canon
  
## Overview

The Air Cavalry serves as the rapid deployment and aerospace assault branch of the [[Verdant Republic Armed Forces]]. Specializing in orbital insertion, atmospheric warfare, air-mobile combat, and expeditionary strike operations, the Air Cavalry is designed to rapidly deploy combat power across planetary and interstellar battlefields.

The branch is known for its speed, flexibility, and aggressive maneuver doctrine.

## Responsibilities
The Air Cavalry is responsible for:
- orbital assault operations
- atmospheric combat
- aerospace strike missions
- rapid troop deployment
- close air support
- aerial reconnaissance
- extraction operations
- deep strike raids

## Command Structure
The Air Cavalry operates under:
- [[Verdant Republic Armed Forces]]
- [[High Strategic Council]]

The branch frequently coordinates with:
- [[Verdant Republic Navy]]
- [[Verdant Republic Ground Forces]]
- [[Republic Special Operations Command]]
  
## Doctrine
Air Cavalry doctrine emphasizes:
- rapid maneuver warfare
- vertical envelopment
- orbital insertion tactics
- aggressive air-mobile operations
- flexible battlefield response
- combined aerospace-ground coordination
  
The branch prioritizes speed and operational adaptability over static defensive warfare.

## Operational Philosophy
The Air Cavalry is built around the principle that:
> rapid force projection can destabilize enemy operations before they can fully respond.

This philosophy has made the branch highly effective during:
- rapid invasions
- planetary reinforcement operations
- counterinsurgency campaigns
- emergency response deployments

- aerospace wings

- tactical support groups
