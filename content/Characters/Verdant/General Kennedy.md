# General Sean Kennedy

## Biography
General Sean Kennedy is the supreme military leader and symbolic face of the [[Verdant Republic]] during its rise as a major power within the Unknown Regions. Known for his disciplined leadership and belief in “winning clean,” Kennedy became one of the most respected wartime commanders in Verdant history.

At the age of six, Kennedy witnessed the assassination of his father, the [[Founder of Verdant]], an event that would permanently shape his view of leadership, sacrifice, and war. Determined to protect the Republic his father created, Kennedy entered the [[Verdant Republic Armed Forces]] at sixteen years old and participated in early campaigns against regional powers hostile to Verdant expansion and political ideology.

During his military service, Kennedy demonstrated exceptional battlefield awareness, leadership capability, and strategic intuition. At the age of twenty, he began studying the ways of the Jedi under the emerging Verdant Force traditions, eventually training to become a Jedi Knight while continuing active military service.

At twenty-two years old, Kennedy was granted command authority over the [[Verdant Republic]] by [[Grand Marshal Lead]], one of his father’s closest allies and senior military advisors. Over the following five years, Kennedy reorganized the Republic’s military structure, modernized its fleet doctrine, and led multiple successful campaigns that transformed Verdant into one of the dominant military powers of the Unknown Regions.

At twenty-seven years old, the first major conflict between the [[Verdant Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] erupted. Although Verdant forces successfully resisted Imperial advances and demonstrated their military strength to the wider galaxy, the war came at tremendous cost to both the Republic and Kennedy personally.

## Personality
Kennedy is widely regarded as calm, disciplined, and strategically minded. Unlike many wartime leaders, he strongly believes military victory should be achieved with restraint rather than unnecessary destruction. His leadership style emphasizes adaptability, professionalism, and personal responsibility toward the soldiers under his command.

Despite his composed public image, Kennedy carries the burden of constant warfare and the pressure of protecting the Republic founded by his family. Those closest to him describe him as pragmatic, emotionally guarded, and relentless when confronting threats to Verdant sovereignty.

## Combat Doctrine
Kennedy is considered one of the Republic’s most dangerous Force-capable military commanders. Unlike traditional Jedi duelists, his combat philosophy was developed around frontline warfare and military command operations.

His lightsaber combat style is heavily influenced by Form V principles, emphasizing aggressive counterattacks, defensive redirection, and overwhelming pressure during close combat engagements. Military analysts often describe Kennedy’s style as highly adaptive, combining disciplined saber combat with rapid tactical decision-making and Force-enhanced battlefield awareness.

Kennedy frequently integrates Force abilities directly into battlefield operations, using telekinetic precision, enhanced reflexes, and precognitive combat awareness to coordinate assaults and disrupt enemy formations.

## Force Abilities

### Known Capabilities
- Advanced precognitive combat awareness
- Force-enhanced speed and reflexes
- High-level telekinetic manipulation
- Defensive blaster redirection
- Shockwave projection
- Multi-target engagement capability

### Limitations
- Avoids excessive destructive Force techniques
- Cannot sustain prolonged Force exertion indefinitely
- Prefers coordinated military operations over isolated combat engagements

## Reputation
Among Republic forces, Kennedy became a symbol of resistance and military unity during the early wars against the Galactic Empire. His presence on the battlefield was known to significantly improve troop morale and stabilize collapsing fronts during critical engagements.

Imperial intelligence reports frequently classified Kennedy as an extremely high-threat Force-capable commander due to his ability to combine Jedi combat proficiency with advanced military leadership.

Enemy forces often referred to Kennedy as:
- “The Verdant General”
- “The Republic Knight”
- “The Unknown Regions Warlord”
