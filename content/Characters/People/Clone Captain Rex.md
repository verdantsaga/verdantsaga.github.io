Captain Rex founded the clone underground network and he help rescue clones from the empire. Once the clones were rescued, he would put them through a network, where they would disappear from the radar of the empire, moving to a life of peace. The disappearing factor is what makes this very interesting Rex was secretly connected to networks used by the insiders. The insiders wanted things like clones and they were willing to offer places where clones could go for safety. Rex would end up sending majority of the clones to the reach (Verdant territory) these clones would remain in peace and some would actually take up arms to fight for this new republic or to train the future generations of the republic soldiers. The most notable one out of all of this is Commander Cody. The insiders eventually got the RSOC to attempt to rescue/bring Captain Rex to the Verdant Republic but once the rescue force reached Rex, he denied the offer.
