Auno Tarnam was a highly respected and calculating Separatist Admiral who served the Confederacy of Independent Systems from the outbreak of the Clone Wars. As the Supreme Commander of the [[9th Outer Rim Armada]], he oversaw a massive, self-contained naval asset composed of 14,000 warships and an immense logistical tail.

Unlike many of his corporate peers, Tarnam was an astute military logician who prioritized tactical independence over rigid adherence to central command guidelines. When the sudden, unexplained central shutdown signal was broadcast from Mustafar, Tarnam immediately suspected a catastrophic compromise. Refusing to let his fleet follow an unverified command so blindly, he deduced that the transmission was either a sophisticated Republic cyber-attack or an internal betrayal by the Separatist leadership.

Recognizing that the vast majority of the Confederate military would comply with the transmission and face immediate execution or dismantling, Tarnam acted instantly. He ordered his technical staff to sever the Armada's central HoloNet relays, locked down the fleet's localized network firewalls to preserve his one trillion droids, and issued an immediate directive for the entire 9th Armada to execute a blind, coordinated hyperspace jump into uncharted territory.
