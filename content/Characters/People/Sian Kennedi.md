## Biography

### Early Life

Sian Kennedi was born on [[Viridion]] in 24 BBY, the son of [[Ruux Kennedi]], the founder of the [[Verdant Republic]].

At the age of six, Sian witnessed the assassination of his father. The death of Ruux left Sian without either of his parents and became one of the defining events of his childhood.

Ruux’s close friend, second-in-command, and highly experienced General [[Jatik Leed]], took Sian in following the deaths of his parents. Leed had served alongside Ruux for years and was one of the few people who fully understood the purpose behind Ruux’s work. Leed knew that Ruux had not simply been building the Verdant Republic for himself; he had been building it for his son.

Leed therefore promised to protect Sian and raise him as his own.

This relationship would have a profound influence on Sian’s later life. Leed became both a guardian and mentor to the young heir of the Republic, while also preserving Ruux’s vision for the future.

### Jedi Training

In 17 BBY, at the age of seven, Sian began his Jedi training under [[Mace Windu]] and [[Rahm Kota]].

His training was unusual due to the combination of his Jedi education and his connection to the Verdant military. Windu provided Sian with a traditional Jedi foundation, while Kota’s experience as a military commander exposed him to a more unconventional approach to Jedi leadership and warfare.

Sian’s development under both Masters would eventually shape his distinctive approach to combat. He became capable of combining Jedi abilities with conventional military command, rather than treating the two roles as separate aspects of his life.

### Military Service

In 8 BBY, at the age of sixteen, Sian joined the [[Verdant Republic Army]].

Despite his youth, Sian quickly demonstrated exceptional battlefield awareness, leadership ability, and strategic intuition. His military service allowed him to apply the lessons he had learned from both Mace Windu and Rahm Kota to conventional warfare.

Sian participated briefly in the [[North Republic War]] in 7 BBY.

Later that same year, he participated in the [[Ssi-ruuvi Imperium War]], which continued into 6 BBY. During the conflict, Sian distinguished himself as a capable young commander and became highly decorated.

By the end of the war, Sian was only eighteen years old.

His accomplishments at such a young age contributed significantly to his growing reputation throughout the Republic and established him as one of its most promising military leaders.

### Darth Seveerus

In 2 BBY, Sian encountered [[Darth Seveerus]], the first Dark Lord he had ever personally faced.

The confrontation became one of the most significant events of Sian’s career. Sian defeated and killed Darth Seveerus, ending his first direct confrontation with a Dark Lord.

Following Seveerus’ defeat, Sian was given the rank of [[General of the Republic]].

Sian became the supreme leader of the Verdant Republic, succeeding the legacy established by his father, Ruux Kennedi.

The significance of Sian’s rise was not lost on Jatik Leed. Leed had been the second person to hold the rank of General of the Republic, following Ruux Kennedi, and had spent years protecting Sian and preserving Ruux’s vision for the Republic.

With Sian’s defeat of Darth Seveerus, the purpose behind Ruux’s work appeared to have finally reached its intended successor.

### General of the Republic

As General of the Republic, Sian became both the supreme military leader and the political face of the Verdant Republic.

His position was particularly significant because of his connection to the Republic’s founding. He was not simply another military commander who had risen through the ranks; he was the son of Ruux Kenned, the Republic’s founder, and had been raised under the protection of one of Ruux’s closest military allies.

Sian’s leadership combined the ideals of his father, the experience of Jatik Leed, and the teachings of his Jedi Masters.

Over time, he became increasingly associated with the identity of the Republic itself.

### Confrontation with Darth Vader

At a later point in his career, Sian confronted [[Darth Vader]].

Sian understood that defeating Vader in a direct one-on-one confrontation was unlikely. Rather than entering the engagement expecting to win, he prepared for the possibility that additional support would be necessary.

Before confronting Vader, Sian positioned another Jedi in the area.

Sian fought Vader directly and managed to hold his own against the [[Sith Lord]]. When the predetermined signal was given, the other Jedi entered the engagement, turning the confrontation into a two-on-one battle.

The fight ultimately ended in a draw.

The confrontation became an important demonstration of Sian’s tactical judgment. Rather than allowing his pride or confidence to dictate the engagement, he recognized the threat Vader represented and planned around it.

## Personality

Sian is widely regarded as calm, disciplined, and strategically minded.

His leadership philosophy emphasizes adaptability, professionalism, and personal responsibility toward the soldiers and civilians under his protection. He strongly believes that military victory should be achieved with restraint rather than unnecessary destruction.

Despite his composed public image, Sian carries the burden of his father’s death, the responsibility of protecting the Republic his father founded, and the expectations placed upon him as its supreme leader.

The death of Ruux at Sian’s young age profoundly shaped his understanding of leadership and sacrifice. Jatik Leed’s influence also taught Sian the importance of responsibility and protecting those entrusted to his command.

Sian is pragmatic when confronting threats to the Republic and is willing to make difficult decisions when necessary.

## Combat Doctrine

Sian is considered one of the Republic’s most dangerous Force-capable military commanders.

Unlike traditional Jedi duelists, his combat philosophy developed around the realities of frontline warfare and military command.

His lightsaber combat style is heavily influenced by [[Form V]] principles, emphasizing aggressive counterattacks, defensive redirection, and overwhelming pressure during close combat engagements.

Sian’s style is highly adaptive, allowing him to combine disciplined lightsaber combat with rapid tactical decision-making and Force-enhanced battlefield awareness.

He frequently integrates his Force abilities directly into battlefield operations rather than treating them solely as tools for individual combat.

## Reputation

By the time of the Republic’s major conflicts with the [[Galactic Empire]], Sian Kennedi had become the symbolic face of the Verdant Republic.

To the people of the Republic, Sian represented both the continuation of Ruux Kennedi’s legacy and the future of the Republic itself.

His position as the son of the Republic’s founder, a Jedi, a decorated military commander, and the General of the Republic made him uniquely recognizable among Verdant’s leadership.

Sian’s presence on the battlefield became associated with resistance, unity, and military strength. His reputation as a commander was further reinforced by his ability to personally participate in combat while continuing to direct larger military operations.

Among Republic forces, Sian’s appearance on the battlefield could significantly improve troop morale and stabilize collapsing fronts during critical engagements.

Imperial intelligence regarded Kennedi as an extremely high-threat Force-capable commander because of his ability to combine Jedi combat proficiency with advanced military leadership.

His reputation extended beyond the Republic itself, with enemies referring to him by titles including:

- The Verdant General
    
- The Republic Knight
    
- The Unknown Regions Warlord
    

## Symbol of the Republic

Sian’s importance to the Verdant Republic extends beyond his military and political authority.

By the time the Republic entered its major conflicts with the Galactic Empire, Sian had become the face of the Republic.

His image represented the survival of the state founded by his father and the continuation of the ideals for which Ruux had fought.

This made Sian an important symbol both domestically and internationally. For Republic citizens and soldiers, he represented continuity and resistance. For the Empire, he represented the leadership and military capability standing between Imperial expansion and the Verdant Republic.

His personal history also strengthened this symbolism. Sian had witnessed the death of his father, been raised by Ruux’s closest military companion, trained as a Jedi, served as a soldier, and eventually inherited the highest military and political position in the Republic.

As a result, Sian was viewed not merely as the Republic’s leader, but as one of the living embodiments of its history.

## Relationships

### Ruux Kennedi

Ruux Kennedi was Sian’s father and the founder of the Verdant Republic.

Sian witnessed Ruux’s assassination when he was six years old.

Ruux’s work would ultimately shape the entirety of Sian’s life. According to Jatik Leed, Ruux had been building the Verdant Republic  with the intention that his son would eventually inherit the legacy he had created.

### Jatik Leed

Jatik Leed was Ruux Kennedi’s close friend, second-in-command, and a highly experienced General.

Following the deaths of Sian’s parents, Leed took Sian in and raised him as his own.

Leed was the second individual to hold the rank of General of the Republic, succeeding Ruux Kennedi.

Of the people who knew Ruux, Leed was one of the few who fully understood that Ruux had been building the Verdant Republic for Sian.

Leed promised to protect Ruux’s son and ensure that Sian survived to inherit the legacy his father had created.

### Mace Windu

Mace Windu was one of Sian’s Jedi Masters.

Windu began training Sian in 17 BBY and provided him with a traditional Jedi foundation.

### Rahm Kota

Rahm Kota was one of Sian’s Jedi Masters.

Kota’s military experience had a significant influence on Sian’s development, particularly his understanding of military leadership and the relationship between Jedi and conventional armed forces.

## Legacy

Sian Kennedi’s legacy is inseparable from that of the Verdant Republic.

The son of its founder, student of two Jedi Masters, decorated soldier, and eventual General of the Republic, Sian came to embody the Republic’s military and political identity.

His rise from a child who witnessed the assassination of his father to the supreme leader of the Republic became one of the defining stories of Verdant history.

By the time the Republic faced its greatest threats, Sian Kennedi was no longer simply Ruux Kennedi’s son.

He had become the face of the Verdant Republic.
