Commander Cody was a highly decorated Clone Marshal Commander who transitioned from a loyal Republic general's right hand into a conflicted Imperial officer haunted by his actions. Cody (designated CC-2224) commanded the 7th Sky Corps and directly led the 212th Attack Battalion alongside Jedi General Obi-Wan Kenobi during the clone wars. In 18 BBY just one year after the fall of the Republic commander Cody went AWOL. Commander Cody ended up going through multiple networks, eventually leading him into the insiders which were looking for clones. Commander Cody was a very experienced clone and was highly valuable to the insiders, eventually, Cody was led to the newly formed verdant republic. Commander Cody would end up taking arms with the verdant republic and would end up being the first clone in Verdant Republic history to reach the rank of general.
