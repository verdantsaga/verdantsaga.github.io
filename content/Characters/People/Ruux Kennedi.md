## Biography

### Viridion War

Ruux Kennedi was the leader of the [[Stirteeja Regime]] during the final stages of the [[Viridion War]] against the [[Totalitarian Regime]].

Under Ruux’s leadership, the Stirteeja forces ultimately defeated the Totalitarian Regime, bringing the war to an end in 18 BBY.

Following the victory, Ruux founded the [[Verdant Republic]], establishing a new government in the aftermath of the war.

### Assassination

Ruux’s leadership of the newly established Republic was short-lived.

Approximately one month after the Stirteeja victory, and only weeks after the establishment of the Verdant Republic, Ruux was assassinated in 18 BBY.

The assassination took place in the capital city of [[Viridion]] during a public ceremony honoring soldiers who had served during the Viridion War. While Ruux was thanking the troops who had fought in the conflict, [[Totalitarian Remnant]] infiltrators carried out the assassination.

Ruux’s death became one of the earliest major tragedies in the history of the Verdant Republic.

His death also left his young son, [[Sian Kennedi]], without his father. Ruux’s close friend and second-in-command, General [[Jatik Leed]], subsequently took Sian in and raised him as his own.

## Legacy

Although Ruux Kennedi’s time as leader of the Verdant Republic was brief, his influence on the Republic’s history was immense.

He led the Stirteeja Regime to victory, founded the Verdant Republic, and established the foundation upon which the Republic would be built.

Ruux’s legacy would later be carried on by his son, Sian Kennedi, who would eventually become the [[General of the Republic]] and supreme leader of the state his father had founded.
