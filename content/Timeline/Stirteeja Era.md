# Stirteeja Era

#history #era #pre-republic #canon

## Overview

The Stirteeja Era was a turbulent period in Verdant history marked by planetary civil war, political upheaval, and the eventual founding of the [[Verdant Republic]].

During this era, the planet [[Viridion]] was divided between three major alliances competing for control of the world. The conflict would ultimately determine the future direction of Verdant civilization and shape the political institutions that still exist today.

The era is named after the [[Stirteeja Regime]], the military-led government that emerged victorious from the conflict and later transformed into the [[Verdant Republic]].

---

## Major Powers

### [[Stirteeja Regime]]
A stratocratic government in which military service and leadership formed the basis of political authority.

Led by [[Founder of Verdant]], the regime believed that lasting peace could only be achieved through military strength, discipline, and centralized authority. The Stirteeja leadership envisioned a future far beyond Viridion, despite lacking the technology to safely travel beyond their own world.

Following victory in the civil war, the regime restructured itself into what would become the [[Verdant Republic]].

### The Totalitarian Military Regime
A highly authoritarian and militarized state that sought complete control over Viridion through fear, surveillance, and absolute state power.

The regime was responsible for the destruction of the Third Alliance and became the primary enemy of the Stirteeja Regime during the latter stages of the war.

Following its defeat, its territories were absorbed into the emerging Verdant Republic.

### The Third Alliance
A coalition of regional governments and local authorities that attempted to remain independent from the two dominant powers.

The alliance was eventually destroyed by the Totalitarian Military Regime during one of the war's most infamous campaigns.

Its destruction shocked much of Viridion's population and generated widespread support for the Stirteeja Regime.

---

## The Civil War

The Verdant Civil War engulfed the entire planet of Viridion and lasted for years. Massive military campaigns devastated entire regions as the three alliances fought for survival and dominance.

A turning point occurred when the Totalitarian Military Regime launched a campaign that resulted in the destruction of the Third Alliance. Public outrage over the massacre shifted support toward the Stirteeja Regime, allowing it to unite much of the remaining population under its banner.

As the war continued, the Stirteeja military steadily gained territory and ultimately defeated its rival, bringing an end to the conflict.

---

## First Contact

During the later stages of the war, off-world visitors arrived on Viridion and shared knowledge of advanced technologies with the Stirteeja leadership.

These exchanges introduced concepts previously unknown to the people of Viridion, including:

- Advanced military technologies
- Interstellar navigation
- Spacecraft engineering
- Galactic political structures

Many of the early designs that would later influence Verdant warships and military equipment can trace their origins to information acquired during this period.

---

## Founding of the Republic

Following victory, [[Founder of Verdant]] dissolved the Stirteeja Regime and established the [[Verdant Republic]].

Officially, this transition marked the beginning of a new era of unity and reconstruction.

However, many events of the transition remained hidden from the public for generations.

---

## The Purges

In the aftermath of the war, large numbers of loyalists associated with the defeated Totalitarian Military Regime were secretly executed under orders from [[Founder of Verdant]].

These actions were concealed from the public and omitted from official historical records.

According to later accounts, the Founder believed that allowing the defeated ideology to survive would eventually lead to another civil war. His decisions were motivated by a desire to secure a safer future for both the Republic and his son, who would one day become [[General Sean Kennedy]].

The morality of these actions remains one of the most controversial subjects in modern Verdant historical scholarship.

---

## Legacy

The Stirteeja Era established many of the foundations of modern Verdant society:

- The creation of the [[Verdant Republic]]
- The rise of the Verdant military tradition
- The first known contact with off-world civilizations
- The beginning of Verdant spaceflight development
- The political legacy of [[Founder of Verdant]]
- The origins of the Kennedy family dynasty

The era officially ended with the founding of the [[Verdant Republic]], though its influence continues to shape Verdant politics and military doctrine centuries later.
