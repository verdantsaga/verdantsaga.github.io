The Auritaan Reach is a pocket of civilizations located within the unknown regions. It contained many organized factions including the [[Verdant Republic]] and the once powerful [[Krenaan Empire]]. The Auritaan Reach was shielded by the Void which stopped factions like the [[Galactic Republic]] and the [[Galactic Empire]] from reaching it.
