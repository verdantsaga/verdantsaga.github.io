#canon 
**9th Outer Rim Armada / CIS Rogue Fleet**

- Total Ships Stolen: 14,000 Warships and Support Vessels
- Heavy Battleships / Dreadnoughts: ~2,100 ships
- Light Frigates / Destroyers: ~6,300 ships
- Lucrehulks / Automated Factory & Cargo Ships: ~5,600 ships
- Total Battle Droids Onboard: 1 Trillion Droids (stored in low-power compression racks across the Lucrehulks and cargo holds).

0.1% of the CIS Navy
1. Heavy Battleships & Dreadnoughts (~2,100 Ships)

These are the frontline shields and heavy hitters of the fleet. They provide the raw firepower needed to protect the more vulnerable cargo and factory ships.

- [Providence-class Carrier/Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Providence-class_carrier/destroyer/Legends) (~600 ships): The backbone of capital ship combat. These sleek, cylindrical warships feature massive hangars for vulture droids and are bristling with heavy turbolaser batteries.

- [Providence-class Dreadnought](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Providence-class_Dreadnought) (~100 ships): The upscaled, 2,100-meter variant of the standard Providence. These serve as the localized command ships for the Neimoidian admiralty. One serves as the Supreme Commander's personal flagship.

- [Subjugator-class Heavy Cruiser](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Subjugator-class_heavy_cruiser) (1–2 rare ships): If your AU fleet was a high-priority sector command, they might possess a rare, scaled-down or surviving prototype sister ship to General Grievous's Malevolence. While it might not have the mega-ion cannon, its massive hull serves as an incredible heavy brawler and a symbol of absolute Separatist defiance.

- [Sabaoth Destroyers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Sabaoth_Destroyer) or Custom Corporate Dreadnoughts (~1,400 ships): Heavy, blocky warships purchased from private corporate militaries or manufactured by the Techno Union to round out the fleet's heavy defensive lines.

2. Light Frigates & Destroyers (~6,300 Ships)

These are the agile skirmishers. They form a protective screen around the fleet, chasing off enemy starfighters and swarming larger Imperial targets with long-range missile and turbolaser fire.

- [Munificent-class Star Frigate](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Munificent-class_star_frigate) (~4,500 ships): The most common ship in the fleet. Though skeletal in appearance, they feature massive prow-mounted heavy twin turbolasers and intense subspace communications arrays. They are excellent for coordinating long-range sensory grids to detect incoming Imperial scouts.

- [Recusant-class Light Destroyer](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Recusant-class_Light_Destroyer) (~1,800 ships): Fully automated, lean, and aggressive warships. They don't have life support systems, making them purely mechanical executioners. Driven by droid brains, they are programmed to form swarming "wolfpacks" to overwhelm enemy capital ships.

3. Lucrehulks, Factory & Cargo Ships (~5,600 Ships)

This is the heart and soul of your rogue faction. Without these, the fleet would starve or run out of fuel within months. In deep space, this logistical wing turns the fleet into a self-sustaining interstellar nation.

- [Lucrehulk-class Battleship](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Lucrehulk-class_battleship) (~400 ships): The legendary, ring-shaped gargantuans of the Trade Federation. While heavily armed with hundreds of point-defense laser cannons, their primary value is their staggering carrying capacity.

- Droid Storage: These 400 ships hold the vast majority of your 1 trillion battle droids, folded tightly into low-power, matrix-style compression racks.

- Lucrehulk-class Core Ships (~200 ships): The detachable spherical centers of the Lucrehulks. They can land directly on planets to deploy massive ground bases or serve as heavily armored central processing hubs for the fleet's droid network.

- [Hardcell-class Interstellar Transports](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hardcell-class_interstellar_transport) (~2,500 ships): Fuel-burning, rocket-shaped vessels originally owned by the Techno Union. Your Neimoidian engineers use these primarily as mobile chemical labs, tech-repair stations, and localized fuel distribution hubs.

- Automated Corporate Ore-Grinders & Foundries (~1,500 ships): Massive, blocky industrial vessels built by the Trade Federation and Mining Guild. They feature colossal tractor beams and plasma saws used to pull in whole asteroids, break them down into raw metals, and feed them directly into automated manufacturing bays to forge replacement parts for the fleet.

- Neimoidian Luxury & Luxury-Cargo Tenders (~1,000 ships): Heavily retrofitted civilian transport ships. Because the warships are almost entirely automated and lack basic comforts, your organic Neimoidian command staff and their living crews live entirely aboard these retrofitted luxury vessels, linking them structurally or keeping them heavily guarded in the absolute center of the fleet's defensive formation.

4. Starfighter & Ground Complement

Tucked inside the massive hangars of the Providence and Lucrehulk classes are the fleet's automated defensive swarms:

- [Vulture Droids](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Variable_Geometry_Self-Propelled_Battle_Droid,_Mark_I) & [Hyena Bombers](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Hyena-class_bomber): Millions of starfighters attached directly to the exterior hull racks and hangar ceilings, ready to launch in seconds if the fleet's perimeter is breached.  

- [Droch-class Boarding Ships](https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/Droch-class_boarding_ship) ("Pod Hunters"): Specialized organic-looking craft used to slice open enemy hulls and inject thousands of B2 Super Battle Droids or MagnaGuards directly into enemy command bridges.
