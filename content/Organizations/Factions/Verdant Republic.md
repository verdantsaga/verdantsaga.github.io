## Overview

The Verdant Republic is a federal stratocratic republic located within the [[Auritaan Reach]]. It was established in 18 BBY following the defeat of the Totalitarian Regime during the Viridion War and the dissolution of the Stirteeja Regime.

The Republic was founded by Ruux Kennedi, who had led the Stirteeja Regime to victory during the final stages of the Viridion War. Rather than continue the wartime government, Kennedi established a new republican state intended to unite the peoples and systems that had supported the Stirteeja cause.

Although the Republic was founded only one year after the end of the Clone Wars, its origins were independent of that conflict. The Republic emerged during the early years of the Galactic Empire, at a time when the new Imperial government was consolidating control over the galaxy.

The Auritaan Reach remained unknown to the Galactic Empire and the wider galaxy because of the Void, which made the region effectively unreachable from the main galaxy. The Republic’s earliest knowledge of galactic events came through General Rahm Kota, the Rogue Separatist Armada, and the secretive network known as the Insiders.

Upon its establishment, the Verdant Republic was already the most powerful faction within the Auritaan Reach. Its initial strength came from the military inherited from the Stirteeja Regime, the forces and resources brought by General Rahm Kota’s element, the Rogue Separatist Armada, and military contributions made by systems that joined the new Republic. Among these contributions were surviving vessels from the former Novstanda Empire fleet.

The Republic subsequently became the principal military and political power within the Auritaan Reach and developed a strong commitment to preventing the rise of governments that resembled the Totalitarian Regime.

## Capital

Viridion became the capital of the Verdant Republic following its establishment in 18 BBY. The planet served as the political center of the new Republic and remained the seat of its central government.

## Government

The Verdant Republic operates as a federal stratocratic republic. Its government combines representative legislative institutions with a highly militarized executive and strategic command structure.

The General of the Republic serves as both head of state and head of government, as well as Commander-in-Chief of the Verdant Republic Military.

The Grand Marshal of the Republic serves as the General’s second-in-command and assists in coordinating the Republic’s strategic institutions.

The Verdant Senate serves as the Republic’s legislative body, while the High Strategic Council coordinates the major strategic functions of the state.

## High Strategic Council

The High Strategic Council consists of senior High Marshals responsible for major areas of national power, including:
- Armed Forces
- War Production
- Intelligence
- Strategic Logistics
- Civil Affairs
- Strategic Science and Advanced Development

The Council provides strategic coordination between the Republic’s military, industrial, intelligence, logistical, scientific, and civil institutions.

## Leadership

General of the Republic

The General of the Republic is the highest executive authority of the Verdant Republic and serves as Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces.

Ruux Kennedi became the first General of the Republic following its establishment in 18 BBY.

Grand Marshal of the Republic

The Grand Marshal of the Republic serves as the General’s second-in-command. The position provides continuity of executive and military leadership and allows the Grand Marshal to coordinate the Republic’s strategic institutions on behalf of the General.

## Formation

Viridion War

The origins of the Verdant Republic can be traced to the Viridion War, which began in 23 BBY between the Stirteeja Regime and the Totalitarian Regime.

The conflict was fought on Viridion. Although other systems within the Auritaan Reach provided resources, personnel, and other forms of assistance to both sides, the war itself did not develop into an interstellar naval conflict. Support from outside systems was not evenly distributed, however, with the majority of assistance going to the Stirteeja Regime.

By the final stages of the war, the Stirteeja military possessed approximately 12–15 billion personnel, while the Totalitarian military possessed approximately 15–18 billion.

The Stirteeja Regime ultimately defeated the Totalitarian Regime. Much of the surviving Stirteeja military infrastructure was subsequently incorporated into the newly established Verdant Republic.

## Founding of the Republic

Following the Stirteeja victory, Ruux Kennedi established the Verdant Republic in 18 BBY.

The new Republic inherited much of the Stirteeja military and administrative infrastructure. It was also strengthened by forces that had reached or gathered within the Auritaan Reach through routes unknown to the wider galaxy.

Among the most significant additions were General Rahm Kota’s forces, the Rogue Separatist Armada, and military assets donated by newly affiliated systems.

Kota’s element contributed experienced personnel, clone forces, and naval assets. The Rogue Separatist Armada provided large numbers of droids and starships. Newly joining systems also transferred military equipment and vessels to the Republic, including surviving ships from the former Novstanda Empire fleet.

The arrival of Kota and the Rogue Separatist Armada was made possible in part through assistance from the secretive Insiders. The Insiders provided intelligence, access to high-value personnel, and advanced technology. Only one member is publicly known: the Jedi who guided Kota through the Void to the safe haven of the Auritaan Reach. The full nature, organization, and extent of the Insiders remain unknown.

As a result, the Verdant Republic possessed a substantial military and naval capability from the moment of its creation.

It rapidly became the most powerful faction within the Auritaan Reach.

## The Empire and the Republic

The founding of the Verdant Republic occurred shortly after the transformation of the Galactic Republic into the Galactic Empire.

Information concerning the fall of the Galactic Republic and the rise of the Empire reached the Auritaan Reach in 19 BBY through General Rahm Kota, his forces, and intelligence obtained through the Insiders.

Because the Auritaan Reach was isolated from the main galaxy by the Void, the Galactic Empire had no knowledge that the region existed. The Republic therefore did not initially face a known Imperial presence within or near its territory.

The information about the Empire was initially kept highly restricted. Only a small number of politically influential individuals were informed of its existence and instructed to keep the information confidential. The Republic’s leadership feared that widespread knowledge of the Empire could cause mass panic and political instability within the Reach.

The Republic nevertheless recognized similarities between the newly established Empire and the Totalitarian Regime that the people of Viridion had recently defeated. The comparison became an important component of Verdant political strategy, although the two regimes were not the same government and the Totalitarian Regime’s conflict had been confined to Viridion.

## The Truth

During the formation of the Republic in 18 BBY, its leadership began selectively revealing information about the Galactic Empire and the destruction of the Galactic Republic.

Rather than portraying the Empire as merely another distant galactic government, Verdant leaders warned that it represented a form of centralized authoritarian rule comparable in certain respects to the system the people of Viridion had fought to overthrow.

The revelation also gave the Republic’s leadership an opportunity to emphasize the importance of unity. The systems of the Auritaan Reach had supported both sides during the Viridion War, although the majority had supported the Stirteeja Regime. The Republic presented its formation as a way to preserve that broader coalition while preventing the political divisions that had allowed the conflict to occur.

Many governments and populations within the Auritaan Reach feared that a power like the Empire could eventually seek access to their region if the Void were ever breached or bypassed. The Verdant Republic consequently presented itself as a guarantee that any external power attempting to impose a similar form of authoritarian rule upon the Reach would be resisted.

This contributed significantly to the early expansion of the Republic.

Systems that feared the possibility of future external interference increasingly viewed membership in the Verdant Republic as a means of collective defense and political stability.

## Mobilization

The Republic’s leadership later used the growing awareness of the Empire to encourage military mobilization.

The government emphasized the possibility that an external power could eventually discover a route into the Auritaan Reach and argued that the Republic required a powerful military capable of defending the region.

The resulting political movement contributed to the expansion of the Verdant Republic Military and helped establish military service as a major component of Verdant society.

The use of information concerning the Empire therefore served two purposes: encouraging systems to join the Republic and generating public support for military expansion.

## Ruux Kennedi’s Assassination

Ruux Kennedi’s leadership of the new Republic lasted only a short time.

Approximately one month after the Republic was established, Kennedi was assassinated by Totalitarian Remnant infiltrators during a ceremony honoring those who had fought in the Viridion War.

The assassination became one of the defining events of early Verdant history and reinforced fears that remnants of the Totalitarian Regime continued to threaten the Republic.

Following Kennedi’s death, Jatik Leed, Kennedi’s close friend and former second-in-command, became a major figure in the early Republic.

## Military

The Verdant Republic Military originated from the combined forces inherited or contributed during the Republic’s foundation.

Its earliest components included:
- Former Stirteeja military forces
- Forces commanded by General Rahm Kota
- Clone personnel associated with Kota’s forces
- Rogue Separatist droid armies
- Rogue Separatist starships
- Naval assets contributed by newly affiliated systems
- Surviving vessels from the former Novstanda Empire fleet
- Technology and intelligence obtained through the Insiders

The resulting military gave the Republic an unusually powerful strategic position within the Auritaan Reach from the beginning of its existence.

As the Republic expanded, its military developed into a unified interstellar force consisting of numerous specialized branches and commands.

These eventually included the Verdant Republic Army, Verdant Republic Navy, Republic Intelligence Command, Republic Special Operations Command, and Expeditionary Threat Command, among others.

## Verdant Jedi Order

The Verdant Jedi Order developed alongside the Republic as an independent Force-sensitive institution.

Following the arrival of [[Rahm Kota]], [[Mace Windu]], [[Tro Qaamel]], and [[Ronen Rozdelyt]], the surviving Jedi in the Auritaan Reach began rebuilding the Jedi tradition.

The Jedi who guided Kota through the Void was the only known member of the Insiders. The Jedi’s role in bringing Kota to the Reach became one of the earliest known connections between the Republic and the wider galaxy, although the Insiders’ broader activities and identity remained concealed.

The Order maintained a close relationship with the Republic and eventually became deeply integrated into Verdant military and civilian society while retaining its own leadership and internal institutions.

The Jedi Order was therefore not a branch of the Republic Military, but a separate institution that cooperated extensively with the Republic.

## Expansion

The Republic’s initial military strength, its ability to unite systems that had supported the Stirteeja cause, and its opposition to authoritarian rule allowed it to attract additional systems throughout the Auritaan Reach.

New members contributed:

- Personnel
- Starships
- Military equipment
- Industrial resources
- Financial resources
- Food and other supplies
- Local defense forces

This process transformed the Republic from a newly established state centered on Viridion into the dominant regional power within the Auritaan Reach.

## Military Doctrine

The Republic’s military doctrine developed from the experience of the Viridion War and the diverse forces that formed the Republic’s original military.

Major principles included:
- Rapid military mobilization
- Large-scale force deployment
- Concentration of firepower
- Strategic logistics
- Interstellar fleet coordination
- Large-scale military production
- Integration of conventional and unconventional forces
- Defense of Republic territory
- Preparedness for external and existential threats
- Protection of the Auritaan Reach from outside powers

The Republic’s experience fighting the Totalitarian Regime also produced a strong cultural belief that military strength was necessary to prevent the return of authoritarian rule.

The possibility that the Galactic Empire or another external power could eventually discover a route through the Void further reinforced the Republic’s emphasis on preparedness.

## Technology and Industry

The Republic inherited substantial industrial infrastructure from the Stirteeja Regime and received additional industrial capabilities from its new member systems.

Its military-industrial development was further accelerated by the acquisition of Separatist technology, naval assets, cloning expertise, and equipment from other civilizations within the Auritaan Reach.

The Insiders also provided the Republic with access to advanced technology, intelligence, and other resources. The full extent and origin of these contributions remain unknown.

Over time, the Republic developed a large military-industrial complex capable of producing starships, weapons, vehicles, armor, droids, and other military equipment on an interstellar scale.

## Status

The Verdant Republic remains one of the principal powers of the Auritaan Reach and serves as the region’s primary military counterweight to external threats.

Because the Galactic Empire and the wider galaxy remain unaware of the Reach’s existence, the Republic’s early history developed largely beyond the knowledge of the main galactic powers. Nevertheless, the possibility that the Reach could eventually be discovered continues to influence Verdant military policy, intelligence operations, and relations with the Insiders.
