# Factions of the Galaxy

#factions #history #canon

## Overview

Numerous governments, alliances, insurgencies, and military organizations have shaped the course of galactic history.

Some factions seek stability and prosperity, while others pursue conquest, independence, or ideological goals. The balance of power between these groups has defined many of the major conflicts of the era.

This page serves as a directory of the major factions active within the galaxy.

---

# Major Powers

## [[Verdant Republic]]

The Verdant Republic is a sovereign interstellar government founded upon principles of representation, security, and unity.

Known for:
- Strong military institutions
- Close integration with the Jedi Order
- Democratic governance
- Strategic expansion
- Advanced military capabilities

The Republic serves as one of the primary political and military powers within the galaxy.

---

## [[Galactic Empire]]

The Galactic Empire is an authoritarian regime that rose following the fall of the Galactic Republic.

Known for:
- Centralized rule
- Massive military strength
- Expansionist policies
- Imperial governance
- Strict enforcement of order

The Empire controls vast regions of galactic territory and remains one of the most powerful forces in existence.

---

## [[Rebel Alliance]]

The Rebel Alliance is a coalition of resistance movements, freedom fighters, and former government officials opposed to Imperial rule.

Known for:
- Guerrilla warfare
- Decentralized operations
- Political resistance
- Fleet raids
- Efforts to restore representative government

The Alliance operates across numerous sectors while fighting against Imperial authority.

---

## [[Separatist Holdouts]]

The Separatist Holdouts are remnants of the former Confederacy of Independent Systems that survived the Clone Wars.

Known for:
- Regional independence movements
- Legacy Separatist technology
- Droid forces
- Isolated strongholds
- Anti-centralized government sentiments

Although fragmented, various Holdout groups continue to operate throughout remote regions of the galaxy.
