# Verdant Republic

## Overview
The Verdant Republic is a major interstellar government formed during the final years of the Clone Wars. Known for its centralized military doctrine, advanced industrial capabilities, and strict strategic governance, the Republic emerged as one of the most powerful factions in the galaxy.
