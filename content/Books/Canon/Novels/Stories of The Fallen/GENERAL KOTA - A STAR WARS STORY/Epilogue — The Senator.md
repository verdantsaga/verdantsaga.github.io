# Epilogue — The Senator

Kalyph — Capital City of Oros

The Empire had arrived.

Imperial transports descended through the atmosphere of Kalyph, carrying thousands of stormtroopers and military personnel. Their arrival had brought the capital of Oros to a standstill. Citizens watched from windows and streets as Imperial forces established their presence throughout the city.

Their objective was straightforward: find General Rahm Kota and determine what had happened to his forces.

Unfortunately for the Empire, Senator Corin Trotz had been less than cooperative.

At the top of the grand staircase leading to the capital building stood Trotz, accompanied by his personal detachment of Kalyph Defense Force soldiers and several members of the Habiran Guard. The latter were among the finest military personnel Kalyph had to offer, assigned exclusively to protect the senator.

An Imperial contingent approached the staircase and stopped several meters from the assembled Kalyph personnel. An Imperial officer stepped forward.

Trotz immediately recognized him.

“I’ve told you before. I don’t know what happened to General Kota, nor do I know where he went,” the senator began. “And I certainly don’t condone this unprovoked visit. Or should I say invasion?”

The officer maintained his composure.

“Please, Senator. We both know you know more than you’re telling us. But there’s no need to waste your breath on me any longer.”

He stepped aside.

“Someone else will be handling this situation.”

Behind the Imperial formation, a shuttle descended and settled onto the landing platform. Its engines powered down, and the ramp slowly lowered.

A figure emerged.

He wore black armor and a dark cape. His mechanical breathing was unmistakable as he began walking toward the capital building.

Trotz watched him approach, uncertain of what he was looking at.

The Imperial officer turned toward the senator.

“Lord Vader.”

Vader continued up the staircase, his attention fixed on Trotz.

“No more lies, Senator. You will tell me what happened to General Kota.”

The Habiran Guard immediately moved closer to Trotz, tightening their protective formation as Vader approached.

“I’ve already told your people that I don’t know—”

Trotz never finished his sentence.

Vader raised his hand, and the senator was suddenly lifted from the ground, his throat caught in an invisible grip.

“Senator!” one of the Habiran Guards shouted.

Without hesitation, both guards rushed forward, raising their specialized electrostaffs and striking at Vader.

Vader released his grip on the senator, allowing him to fall, and ignited his lightsaber.

The first guard struck.

Vader effortlessly intercepted the attack, turning his blade against the second guard as he attempted to flank him. Their weapons clashed against his lightsaber, but the Dark Lord barely moved.

One of the guards was suddenly thrown backward by an invisible force.

The other continued fighting.

“Get the senator inside!” a KDF soldier shouted.

Two soldiers rushed forward, grabbing Trotz and pulling him away from the confrontation. Another KDF trooper instinctively opened fire toward the Imperial formation, and within seconds, the staircase erupted into a firefight.

KDF soldiers exchanged blaster fire with Imperial stormtroopers as both sides scrambled for cover.

As Trotz was rushed through the entrance, he briefly looked over his shoulder.

He saw Vader standing in the middle of the staircase, holding one of the Habiran Guards suspended in the air with the Force while continuing to duel the other.

For a brief moment, their eyes met.

Then the senator disappeared inside the building.

The KDF personnel hurried Trotz down the corridors of the capital building and into a secured room. The chamber had been designated for emergencies and included an exit leading to an open outdoor area large enough to accommodate evacuation transports.

The senator was still trying to process what he had witnessed.

A KDF soldier turned toward him.

“Senator, we don’t have much time. We need to authorize an emergency evacuation immediately.”

Trotz looked at him, still shaken.

“Yes. Yes, of course. Call it in.”

The soldier immediately activated his communications device.

“Command, this is Senatorial Security. Initiating emergency evacuation protocol. Prepare the extraction site.”

Outside, KDF personnel began deploying throughout the surrounding area. Defensive positions were established, vehicles were moved into place, and soldiers took up positions around the evacuation perimeter.

The Empire was advancing, and the Kalyph Defense Force was preparing to resist.

Trotz watched as his personnel worked frantically to organize the evacuation. He knew the situation was deteriorating, but he had little idea just how quickly it was happening.

Then came the sound of blaster fire from somewhere down the corridor.

A soldier turned toward the entrance.

“Did you hear that?”

Before anyone could respond, another burst of blaster fire echoed through the building.

Vader was coming.

The evacuation transports arrived shortly afterward.

KDF personnel began coordinating the extraction, directing troops and preparing to move the senator.

Trotz was escorted toward the exit by several soldiers. They had barely reached the outdoor area when a transmission came through their communications channel.

“Evacuation site, this is perimeter command! Imperials have breached our positions! I repeat, Imperials have breached the evacuation site!”

The KDF soldiers immediately raised their weapons.

“Where?” one of them shouted into his communicator.

The response was interrupted by another transmission.

“WE’RE LOSING CONTROL OF THE EVACUATION SITE! I REPEAT, WE’RE LOSING CONTROL!”

Trotz stepped outside.

What he saw was chaos.

Imperial stormtroopers were pushing through the KDF defensive perimeter, exchanging blaster fire with defenders who were desperately attempting to hold their positions.

One of the hovering transports attempted to lift off, but a missile struck its side.

The craft exploded, crashing into the evacuation area and sending debris scattering across the ground.

Other transports attempted to escape, but Imperial fire quickly brought them down or forced them to abandon their approach.

“WE’VE LOST THE EVACUATION SITE! ALL UNITS, FALL BACK! FALL BACK!”

The transmission ended.

Trotz stared at the destruction around him. The evacuation had failed. His soldiers were being overwhelmed, and there was no longer any realistic possibility of getting him off the planet.

He turned toward the KDF personnel surrounding him.

“Senator, we need to move!” one of them shouted.

Trotz shook his head.

“No. Look around us.”

“Sir?”

“We’re not getting out of here.”

The soldier looked toward the advancing Imperial forces before turning back to the senator.

Trotz took a breath.

“Get into position. We’re holding this ground.”

“Senator, we can still—”

“That’s an order.”

The KDF soldiers exchanged brief glances before taking up defensive positions around him.

They would make their final stand.

The fighting continued.

Imperial stormtroopers advanced through the evacuation site, systematically eliminating the remaining KDF resistance. The defenders fought desperately, but their positions were collapsing one after another.

Then, through the smoke and blaster fire, a familiar figure emerged from the capital building.

Darth Vader.

He walked steadily toward the senator’s position, his lightsaber cutting through anyone foolish enough to stand in his way.

KDF soldiers opened fire.

Vader deflected their shots, sending blaster bolts back into the advancing defenders. One soldier fell, then another. The remaining troops attempted to reorganize their defensive line, but Vader continued advancing without hesitation.

Trotz watched as the soldiers protecting him were overwhelmed.

One of them fell only a few meters away.

Another was struck down before he could raise his weapon.

The senator looked around.

There was no escape.

A KDF soldier beside him reached for his sidearm and offered it to Trotz.

“Senator. Take this.”

Trotz accepted the blaster pistol.

Vader continued approaching.

The senator raised the weapon and fired.

Vader effortlessly deflected the bolt with his lightsaber. The redirected shot struck Trotz in the arm.

He cried out in pain, dropping the pistol as he stumbled backward. His other hand immediately moved to clutch the wound.

Vader stopped directly in front of him.

The senator looked up.

“Where is General Kota?” Vader demanded.

Trotz struggled to catch his breath.

He looked at the Dark Lord of the Sith, then past him at the destruction surrounding them.

Despite everything, he managed a faint smile.

“You’ll never find him,” he said. “He’ll find you.”

Vader stared at him.

“I hope he does.”

The Dark Lord raised his hand.

Trotz was lifted from the ground once again, his body suspended as Vader tightened his grip around the senator’s throat.

The senator struggled briefly, but there was nothing he could do.

His resistance slowly faded.

A moment later, his body went limp.

Vader released him.

Senator Corin Trotz fell lifelessly to the ground.

The Dark Lord turned away, his mechanical breathing the only sound accompanying the advancing Imperial forces.

Behind him, the Kalyph Defense Force’s resistance was collapsing.

The Empire had come looking for a Jedi General.

It would leave with control of an entire world.

Kalyph had fallen.
