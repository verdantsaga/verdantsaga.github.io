# Republic Intelligence Bureau

#government #intelligence #classified #canon

## Overview
The Republic Intelligence Bureau (RIB) serves as the primary civilian intelligence and internal security agency of the [[Verdant Republic]]. Responsible for counterespionage, internal investigations, anti-corruption operations, political security, and domestic threat monitoring, the Bureau operates as one of the Republic’s most secretive governmental organizations.

The Bureau works to preserve internal stability during periods of growing military expansion and rising external threats.

## Responsibilities
The Republic Intelligence Bureau is responsible for:
- counterespionage
- internal investigations
- anti-corruption enforcement
- political security
- domestic intelligence gathering
- counter-subversion operations
- internal surveillance
- extremist threat monitoring

## Authority
The Bureau operates under the authority of:
- [[Verdant Senate]]

and maintains wartime coordination with:
- [[High Strategic Council]]

## Operational Philosophy
The Bureau prioritizes:
- internal stability
- information control
- counter-infiltration
- national security
- preservation of Republic institutions

Due to the increasing threat posed by the [[Galactic Empire]], the Bureau has expanded significantly during recent wartime periods.

## Relationship With The Military
Although separate from the [[Verdant Republic Armed Forces]], the Bureau frequently cooperates with:
- [[Republic Intelligence Command]]
- [[Republic Special Operations Command]]

Joint operations often involve:
- counterintelligence
- anti-infiltration
- strategic security investigations

## Political Controversy
The Bureau remains politically controversial within portions of the Senate due to concerns regarding:
- surveillance authority
- wartime emergency powers
- classified investigations
- civilian privacy restrictions

Several political factions have accused the Bureau of expanding beyond its original mandate during periods of heightened conflict.

## Known Divisions
- [[Internal Security Division]]
- [[Counterespionage Office]]
- [[Political Investigations Bureau]]
- [[Strategic Surveillance Directorate]]

## Reputation
The Republic Intelligence Bureau is known for:
- secrecy
- political influence
- investigative authority
- aggressive counterintelligence methods

Many Republic citizens view the organization with a mixture of trust, fear, and suspicion.

## Relationship With General Kennedy
The Bureau maintains a cautious relationship with [[General Kennedy]] and portions of the military high command due to disagreements regarding intelligence oversight and wartime authority.
