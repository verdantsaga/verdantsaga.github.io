# High Strategic Council

## Overview
The High Strategic Council (HSC) serves as the supreme strategic command authority of the [[Verdant Republic]]. Established during the later stages of the Clone Wars, the Council coordinates military operations, industrial production, intelligence oversight, and long-term national strategy.

The Council exists to ensure unified wartime decision-making across all branches of the Republic government and armed forces.

## Purpose
The Council is responsible for:
- military strategy
- emergency wartime authority
- industrial mobilization
- defense policy
- classified operations oversight
- interstellar security coordination

## Authority
The High Strategic Council possesses broad emergency powers during periods of conflict and may override standard Senate procedures under authorized wartime conditions.

## Relationship With The Senate
While officially subordinate to the [[Verdant Senate]], the Council often operates with significant autonomy due to the Republic's reliance on centralized military leadership.

This has occasionally created political tensions regarding:
- civilian oversight
- military influence
- emergency authority
- classified operations
