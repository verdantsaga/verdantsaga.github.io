****

The Verdant-class Star Destroyer II was developed by the Verdant Republic as a modernization of the Imperial II-class Star Destroyer. While retaining the intimidating wedge-shaped hull and heavy firepower of its predecessor, the class emphasized survivability, redundancy, aerospace operations, and fleet coordination. Enhanced broadside batteries, expanded carrier facilities, dedicated anti-starfighter defenses, and extensive internal compartmentalization transformed the vessel into one of the most capable fleet command ships in Verdant service.

Primary strengths:
- Exceptional survivability
- Large aerospace wing
- Powerful broadside firepower
- Strong anti-starfighter defenses
- Redundant command systems
- Rapid troop deployment capability

Primary weakness:
- Higher construction and maintenance costs than a standard Imperial II-class Star Destroyer.
