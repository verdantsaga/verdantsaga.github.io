****

The Thorn-class Destroyer served as the primary fleet escort vessel of the Verdant Republic Navy. Unlike the multi-role warships favored by the Galactic Empire, the Thorn was designed around a single objective: protecting larger capital ships from modern battlefield threats.

As missile technology, starfighters, electronic warfare systems, and fast attack craft became increasingly dangerous, Verdant naval planners determined that dedicated escort ships would be more effective than attempting to make every vessel perform every role. The Thorn-class emerged as the solution.

Characteristics

The Thorn-class sacrificed troop capacity, command facilities, and heavy bombardment weaponry in favor of speed, sensors, defensive systems, and fleet protection capabilities.

Its advanced sensor suites, missile-tracking systems, and electronic warfare arrays allowed it to detect and counter threats before they could reach larger vessels. Combined with one of the most extensive point-defense networks ever installed on a destroyer-sized hull, the class became the Republic’s premier screening vessel.

Although armed with gravimetric cannons, turbolasers, and ion weapons, these systems were intended primarily for defensive combat and escort operations rather than fleet-breaking engagements.
