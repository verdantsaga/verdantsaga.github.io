****

The V-50 Reaper Vine heavy strike fighter was developed as the [[Verdant Republic]]’s premier deep-strike and anti-ship aerospace platform. Combining exceptional payload capacity, heavy shielding, and substantial armor protection, the Reaper Vine was capable of engaging targets ranging from armored ground formations to capital ships.

Unlike the lighter [[V-21 Thorn interceptor]] or the versatile [[VF-18 Warden multirole starfighter]], the Reaper Vine prioritized destructive capability and battlefield endurance. Its heavily reinforced airframe, advanced targeting systems, and extensive ordnance capacity made it a favored platform for strategic attack missions and planetary assault operations.

Reaper Vine squadrons were often deployed alongside Thorn interceptors and Warden multirole fighters, forming the backbone of Verdant Republic aerospace strike groups throughout the Republic’s expansion into the Unknown Regions.
