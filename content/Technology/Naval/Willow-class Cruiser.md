****

The Willow-class Cruiser served as the primary general-purpose capital ship of the Verdant Republic Navy. Designed to bridge the gap between the Thorn-class Destroyer and the Ironwood-class Heavy Cruiser, the Willow combined affordability, flexibility, and endurance into a single multi-role platform capable of operating throughout Republic territory.

Where the Ironwood formed the backbone of sector-level operations, the Willow became the backbone of fleet-level operations. Thousands of Willow-class cruisers patrolled trade routes, reinforced frontier systems, escorted larger warships, and conducted anti-piracy operations across Republic space.

Characteristics

The Willow-class was designed around three core principles:

- Flexibility
- Affordability
- Endurance

Unlike specialized warships, the Willow could perform nearly any mission assigned to it. Its balanced weapons package allowed it to engage enemy cruisers, support fleet operations, provide orbital fire support, and conduct independent patrols.

The class carried four medium gravimetric cannons, giving it significantly greater striking power than destroyers while remaining substantially less expensive than heavy cruisers and battlecruisers.
