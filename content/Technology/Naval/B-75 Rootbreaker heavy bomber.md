The B-75 Rootbreaker heavy bomber served as the [[Verdant Republic]]’s primary strategic bombing platform. Developed to destroy heavily fortified military targets, enemy shipyards, planetary defenses, and capital ships, the Rootbreaker emphasized payload capacity, survivability, and long-range operational endurance over speed and maneuverability.

Unlike the [[V-50 Reaper Vine heavy strike fighter]], which operated alongside frontline fighter squadrons, the B-75 was designed to attack strategic objectives deep within hostile territory. Rootbreaker squadrons commonly operated under escort from [[V-21 Thorn interceptor]]s and [[VF-18 Warden multirole starfighter]]s.

The bomber’s immense internal ordnance bays allowed it to carry enough munitions to devastate hardened military installations, making it one of the most feared aerospace assets in the Verdant Republic arsenal.
