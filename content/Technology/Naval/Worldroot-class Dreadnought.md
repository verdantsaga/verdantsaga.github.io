****

The Worldroot-class Dreadnought was one of the largest and most powerful warships ever fielded by the Verdant Republic Navy. Designed to bridge the gap between the Verdant-II Star Destroyer and the Verdant Super Star Destroyer, the class was created specifically to destroy enemy battlefleets without requiring deployment of the Republic’s limited number of strategic supercapital vessels.

Unlike many contemporary warships that emphasized command facilities, troop transport capability, or fighter operations, the Worldroot was engineered primarily for fleet warfare. Its enormous gravimetric weapon systems, exceptional survivability, and powerful command facilities made it one of the most feared vessels in Republic service.

Characteristics

The defining feature of the Worldroot-class was its Gravimetric Siege Array. Consisting of twenty-four heavy gravimetric cannons, the system was capable of collapsing shield networks, fracturing armor structures, and devastating enemy capital ships at long range.

Supporting this primary battery were hundreds of heavy turbolasers, ion cannons, missile launch systems, and one of the largest defensive weapon networks ever mounted aboard a Republic vessel.

Although capable of carrying a significant fighter contingent, the Worldroot was not designed as a carrier. Its aerospace wing existed primarily to support fleet combat operations and protect the dreadnought during extended engagements.

Operational Role

The Worldroot-class served in four primary roles:

- Fleet Breaker
- Campaign Flagship
- Regional Deterrent
- Strategic Assault Platform

A single Worldroot was expected to engage and defeat formations consisting of multiple Star Destroyers, battlecruisers, heavy cruisers, and carrier groups simultaneously.

In many regions of Republic space, a Worldroot represented the most powerful military asset permanently assigned to a sector.

Command Facilities

Unlike smaller warships, the Worldroot possessed extensive command and control infrastructure, including:

- Fleet Command Center
- Strategic Planning Complexes
- Intelligence Operations Center
- Long-Range Communications Hub
- Flag Facilities for Senior Admirals

These systems allowed a Worldroot to coordinate entire regional military campaigns independently.

Typical Battlegroup

A standard Worldroot Battlegroup typically consisted of:

- 1 Worldroot-class Dreadnought (Flagship)
- 3 Sentinel-class Battlecruisers
- 6 Ironwood-class Heavy Cruisers
- 12 Thorn-class Destroyers
- 18 Guardian-class Frigates
- 2 Arbor-class Fleet Carriers
- Fleet Tenders
- Replenishment Ships
- Hospital Ships

Such formations represented some of the largest operational fleet concentrations maintained by the Verdant Republic Navy.

Reputation

Among Verdant naval personnel, a common saying described the vessel’s role:

“An Ironwood secures a system.  
  A Sentinel secures a sector.  
  A Verdant-II secures a campaign.  
  A Worldroot secures victory.”

Imperial naval officers regarded the Worldroot-class as one of the most dangerous warships in the galaxy. While smaller than a Verdant Super Star Destroyer, the Worldroot was built not as a symbol of power, but as a dedicated fleet-killing machine designed to defeat the strongest naval formations the galaxy could field.
